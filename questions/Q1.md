## Formalization

The [Lean formalization](../lean/DepDis/Q1/README.md) covers finite and
unrestricted co-r.e.-completeness, hardness for the fixed consequent with
the stated premise-width and value-bound restrictions, and the absence
of a computable bound on finite counterteam size.

The principal results are in
[Complexity.lean](../lean/DepDis/Q1/Complexity.lean) and
[FinalModelBound.lean](../lean/DepDis/Q1/FinalModelBound.lean).
[Check.lean](../lean/Check.lean) audits their axiom dependencies along with
the main intermediate results. Human review of the mathematical statements
and formalization is recorded separately below.
